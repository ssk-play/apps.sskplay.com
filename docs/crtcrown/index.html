CrtCrown

A CRT monitor frame that sits on your Mac screen, with live scanlines, barrel distortion, and bloom on the window underneath.

Download for macOS Version 1.0.0  ·  692 KB  ·  2026-08-23

CrtCrown needs Screen Recording permission — it reads the pixels of the window beneath the frame in order to draw the CRT effect on them. macOS will ask on first launch.

Nothing captured ever leaves your Mac: the pixels go straight to the on-device renderer and are never written to disk or sent anywhere.

What it is

CrtCrown draws a CRT monitor frame as a floating window on your desktop. Anything that sits under it — a terminal, an emulator, a video, a text editor — is re-rendered in real time through a Metal shader that adds the things a cathode-ray tube did to a picture: scanlines, a curved glass bulge, glow around bright pixels, and a slight bloom that spills into the dark.

It is a display effect, not a video filter. The window underneath keeps working normally — you can type in it, scroll it, and click through it while it looks like it is coming out of a 1987 monitor.

Features

Real-time CRT shader

Scanlines, barrel distortion, and bloom applied live through Metal — not a static overlay image.

Window binding

Bind the frame to a specific window so it follows that window as you move it around.

Seven presets

From a clean Classic CRT to a blown-out Cyberpunk look. Switch between them from the control bar.

Runs on anything current

Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 14 or later. Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

Install

  1. Open the downloaded DMG.
  2. Drag CrtCrown to your Applications folder.
  3. Launch it and allow Screen Recording when macOS asks.

Optional: the window-binding feature also asks for Accessibility permission, which lets CrtCrown move a bound window. Everything else works without it.

Trial and licensing

CrtCrown is free to try for 7 days, with every preset unlocked. After the trial ends the app keeps working — three presets stay free forever, and the four premium presets need a license key.

Buy a license key

After buying, the key arrives by email. Enter it in the app under menu > License… to unlock the premium presets. One key activates the app, and a licensed copy keeps working offline.
